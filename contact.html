Preserving Your Weaves

Saree Care 101

1. Ilkal & Handloom Silk Sarees

We recommend dry-cleaning for the first 2 washes to seal the natural vegetable dyes and maintain the silk sheen of the Tope Teni pallu. For later washes, use gentle cold hand-washing with mild liquid detergent. Never wring or dry in direct harsh sunlight.

2. Everyday Cottons & Linens

Hand wash or delicate machine wash in cold water using a mild soap. If you prefer a crisp, structured drape, light starch can be applied. Iron while slightly damp on medium setting.

3. Storage & Folding Advice

Store handloom sarees wrapped in a pure cotton or muslin fabric. Change the fold creases every 3–4 months to avoid permanent stress marks along the zari. Keep cedar balls or dried neem leaves in your wardrobe instead of harsh chemical mothballs.
