A Note from the Founder

Turning uncertainty into passion

"My journey with NOOLU began during a time of uncertainty. When I lost my job, I found myself wondering what the next chapter of my life would look like."

"I decided to take a step forward and create an opportunity of my own. That’s when the idea of building a saree brand began. What started as a small idea soon became NOOLU — a journey driven by passion, creativity, and the desire to build something authentic."

"From everyday grace to special occasions, we can't wait to bring you sarees that feel as beautiful as they look."

With love, Bhoomika Founder & Curator, NOOLU
